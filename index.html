The programme

A 12-month journey, six gates, one clear path

Every participant moves through the same six stages, delivered across a national network of university and polytechnic labs.

GATE 01

Discover

Aug – Sept

National call opens and real challenges are mapped with partner organisations.

GATE 02

Learn

Sept – Oct

AI foundations and hands-on lab training — no prior experience needed.

GATE 03

Build

Oct – Nov

Design thinking and MVP development, turning ideas into working prototypes.

GATE 04

Compete

December

The National AI Sprint. The strongest 30 teams advance to Venture.

GATE 05

Venture

Jan – Feb

Business coaching and mentoring shape prototypes into real ventures.

GATE 06

Launch

Mar – Apr

Pitch day. The top 10 startups receive seed funding and incubation handover.
